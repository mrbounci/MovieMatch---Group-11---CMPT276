How it works

  1. Start a session

    Give it a name and a short note, like "Friday night, nothing over two hours". You're the host, so only you can edit it or reset the votes.

  2. Everyone suggests a movie

    Type a title and we'll fill in the poster and genre for you. Add a blurb to make your case.

  3. Vote, then watch the winner

    Each session has a voting page. The movie with the most votes rises to the top, and you can leave a review afterwards.
